## About Artan Basha

Artan is an innovative furniture maker based in Tirana, Albania, who blends artistic training with self-taught woodworking expertise. After attending the Artistic Lyceum of Painting, destiny guided him back to his true calling—creative woodworking.

### Expertise

- Handcrafted wooden furniture from natural materials
- Tables, benches, shelves, and sculptural wood accents
- Premium organic wood oil finishes
- Specializes in walnut, oak, and native wood species
- Work highly sought by architects and interior designers

### Notable Works

- Tattoobench (signature piece)
- Deer wood plates in oak
- Walnut trays
- Massive walnut mirror
- Hand-finished cigar ashtray

### Career

- **Started:** 2013
- **Teaching:** Since 2019
- **Languages:** Albanian, German, English, Italian

All pieces are crafted with meticulous attention to detail and finished with organic treatments that enhance the natural beauty of the wood.
