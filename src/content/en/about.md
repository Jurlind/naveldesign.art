## About Artan Basha - Test

Artan Basha developed his artistic passion through formal study at the Artistic Lyceum of Painting before pursuing Finance and Food Technology. His transition into woodworking came through self-directed learning via online resources. He established a prominent workshop in Tirana that attracts commissions from architects and interior designers.

His philosophy centers on bringing nature indoors. As he explains: "The rich contours of wooden edges give tables, benches, shelves, and other room accents a sculptured quality." He believes people should strive for nature-friendly living spaces since most time is spent indoors.

**Career:** Began in 2013 | Started teaching in 2019

## In His Own Words

### First Creation

An oval wooden plate, handturned from pruned olive branches and preserved by his wife—marking the beginning of his woodworking journey.

### Creative Inspiration

"I like that natural variety in patterns creates pure uniformity," he notes. Each tree and branch possesses unique natural designs that inspire his work.

### Favorite Technique

"The final step of the process, which is wood finishing." This is where he observes the wood's character and applies organic oils to enhance its natural beauty and bring out its unique qualities.

### A Memorable Moment

A difficult experience when a customer requested purchase of emotionally significant pieces he'd created for display only. The work was so personally meaningful that parting with them felt like being "ripped away" from them.

## Signature Pieces

* **Tattoobench:** Solid wood stool featuring engraved patterns—a signature piece representing years of craft excellence
* **Deer Wood Plates:** Oak specimens with festive designs, finished in food-safe oil for both beauty and functionality
* **Walnut Trays:** Single-piece European walnut commissions with organic finishing, sought after by collectors and designers
* **Massive Walnut Mirror:** Frame crafted from a 50+ year-old tree, featuring signature dark coloring that speaks to age and character
* **Cigar Ashtray:** Hand-carved solid walnut, demonstrating his precision in detail work

All pieces are crafted with meticulous attention to detail and finished with organic treatments that enhance the natural beauty of the wood.
